# Code of Conduct

We want Aletheia to be a friendly, respectful place for everyone who uses or contributes to it.

**Expected:** be kind and patient, assume good intent, give and accept constructive feedback, and keep discussion about the work.

**Not acceptable:** harassment, personal attacks, discrimination, publishing other people's private information, or sharing credentials and personal financial data.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, issues and contributions that break these rules, and may block repeat offenders. To report a problem, use the [private reporting form](https://github.com/AbsSadhu/Aletheia/security/advisories/new) and mention it is a conduct concern.

This policy is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org), version 2.1.
